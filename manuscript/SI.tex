\noindent This Supplementary Information collects supplementary \emph{results}
that support the main text. The full methodology (Composite Hazard Score and
Habitable Area Fraction, climate--ocean emulator, calibration, surface-water
hazard field, and uncertainty decomposition) is given in the Methods section of
the main text. All quantities are from the calibrated run (seed 0, 400 particles,
two-box climate core), regenerated by the public pipeline.
